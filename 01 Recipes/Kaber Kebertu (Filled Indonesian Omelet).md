# Kaber Kebertu (Filled Indonesian Omelet)

> Kaber Kebertu is a traditional Indonesian stuffed omelet from Beb Vuyk's Groot Indonesisch Kookboek. The omelet is filled with a spiced mixture of meat, chicken, fish or shrimp seasoned with a fragrant bumbu of coriander, cumin, turmeric, sambal and terasi, enriched with coconut cream. The result is a juicy omelet with a rich and aromatic filling.

## Ingrediënten
- 4 eggs
- 100 grams of minced meat, finely chopped chicken, fish or shrimp meat
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1/8 block of [[santen]] (coconut cream)
- 2 tablespoons chopped onions
- 1/2 teaspoon [[ketumbar]] (coriander seeds)
- 1/4 teaspoon [[jinten]] (cumin)
- 1 teaspoon [[sambal ulek]] (chili salsa)
- 1/2 teaspoon [[kunyit]] (turmeric)
- 1/2 teaspoon [[terasi]] (fermented shrimp paste)
- pepper
- salt
- dash of milk
- spring onions (optional)

## Bereiding
1. Grind the onions with the [[ketumbar]], [[jinten]], [[sambal ulek]], [[kunyit]], [[terasi]], salt and pepper into a smooth bumbu (spice paste), using a mortar or immersion blender.
2. Add the bumbu to a wok and sauté it in a few tablespoons of oil.
3. Add the finely chopped meat, chicken, fish or shrimp and cook for about a minute.
4. Add the [[santen]] and 3 tablespoons of water and let everything simmer until the filling is almost done and most of the moisture has evaporated.
5. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt, pepper and a dash of milk until slightly foamy.
6. Heat a little oil in a pan over low heat and pour in the egg mixture.
7. Optionally add some chopped spring onions to the omelet.
8. Let the omelet cook slowly. When the edges start to firm up but the inside is still wet, spoon the filling on top.
9. Carefully fold and roll the omelet over the filling using a spatula until it is closed.
10. Fry the rolled omelet gently for about half a minute on each side until lightly browned.
11. Cut into broad strips and serve.
