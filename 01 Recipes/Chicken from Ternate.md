# Chicken from Ternate

> A creamy but spicy chicken in a reduced coconut sauce, originally from the North Moluccan island of Ternate. This authentic recipe from Beb Vuyk's Groot Indonesisch kookboek features a fragrant bumbu (herb paste) with sambal terasi, candlenuts and lemongrass, simmered in rich coconut cream. Best served with white rice and a cucumber salad.

## Ingrediënten
- 1 chicken of 1000 to 1200 grams
- 1/6 block [[santen]] (coconut cream)
- 2 tablespoons of oil
- 1/4 liter of [[water]]
- 5 tablespoons of chopped onions
- 2 shredded cloves of garlic
- 2 teaspoons of [[sambal terasi]] (chili salsa with shrimp paste)
- 5 puffed [[kemirie]]s (candle nuts)
- 1 teaspoon of ginger powder
- 1 stalk [[sereh]] (lemon grass)
- juice of 1 lemon

## Bereiding
1. Cut the chicken into big pieces.
2. Rub onions, garlic, sambal, puffed [[kemirie]]s and ginger powder together into a paste.
3. Stir-fry the paste in the oil until the onions are yellow.
4. Add 1/4 liter of boiling [[water]] and the [[sereh]] (lemongrass).
5. Simmer the pieces of chicken in this mix until they are halfway done.
6. Add the [[santen]] (coconut cream) and simmer until the sauce has thickened and the chicken is done.
7. Stir the lemon juice through carefully before serving.
