# Lembaran of Chicken - Spicy Indonesian Chicken Dish

> Lembaran of Chicken is a spicy Indonesian chicken dish inspired by chicken kalio. It is rich in flavor thanks to sambal terasi (fermented shrimp paste) and tamarind water, which give it a deep umami taste with a fresh, tangy note. The chicken is cooked in coconut cream with aromatic spices and leaves, resulting in a tender and flavorful dish. It can also be prepared a day in advance for even more tenderness.

## Ingrediënten
- 1 chicken of 1000 to 1200 grams
- 1/6 block of [[santen]] (coconut cream)
- 1/2 liter of [[water]]
- 5 tablespoons of chopped onions
- 2 chopped garlic cloves
- 1 teaspoon of [[sambal terasi]] (trassi - fermented shrimp paste)
- 1 teaspoon [[kunjit]] (turmeric)
- 1 teaspoon of [[laos]] (galangal)
- 3 tablespoons of asem (tamarind) water, made from a piece of asem the size of one walnut
- 1 salam leaf (Asian bay leaf)
- 1 jeruk purut leaf (kaffir lime)
- salt

## Bereiding
1. Cut the chicken into large pieces.
2. Rub onions, garlic, [[sambal terasi]], [[laos]] and [[kunjit]] together into a paste (bumbu). Add salt while rubbing to help grind the herbs into a creamy paste.
3. Add the chicken to the [[water]] with a pinch of salt in a pan and heat slowly on the stove with the lid on.
4. After 10 minutes, add the rubbed spice paste (bumbu), the piece of [[santen]], and the salam and kaffir lime leaves.
5. Let the chicken cook until done, simmering for at least another 25 minutes.
6. Add 3 tablespoons of tamarind [[water]] and simmer for a few more minutes.
7. Serve with white rice and sambal goreng tomato.
