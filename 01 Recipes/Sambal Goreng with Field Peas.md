# Sambal Goreng with Field Peas

> Sambal Goreng with Field Peas is an authentic Indonesian dish from Beb Vuyk's Groot Indonesisch Kookboek. Soft, nutty field peas are simmered in a fragrant spiced coconut milk sauce with lemongrass, salam leaf and galangal. The dish can easily be made vegetarian by omitting the shrimp paste (trassi). Best served with white rice and a sour acar or vegetable dish.

## Ingrediënten
- 1/4 kg of boiled [[field peas]] (fresh or canned)
- 3 tablespoons of sliced onions
- 1 clove of sliced garlic
- 1 teaspoon [[sambal terasi]] (or 1 teaspoon sambal oelek and 1/2 teaspoon terasi)
- 1 teaspoon of laos (galangal)
- 1 teaspoon of Javanese sugar (gula Jawa)
- 1 stalk of sereh (lemongrass)
- 1 salam leaf
- 1/8 block of santen (coconut cream) or 100 ml coconut milk
- 2 tablespoons of oil
- some salt

## Bereiding
1. Rub onions, garlic, sambal, [[laos]], sugar and salt together in a mortar to make a smooth paste.
2. Sauté the spice paste in the oil until the onions turn yellow.
3. Add a few tablespoons of liquid from the can and the coconut milk (or [[santen]] block).
4. Add the lemongrass ([[sereh]]), [[field peas]] and [[salam]] leaf.
5. Simmer on low heat for about 5 minutes until the sauce reduces slightly and the oil from the [[santen]] floats to the surface.
6. Remove the [[sereh]] and [[salam]] leaf before serving.
7. Optionally sprinkle a few drops of lemon juice over the dish just before serving.
