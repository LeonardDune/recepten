# Trancam Salad with Radish and Kencur

> Trancam is a crispy and spicy Indonesian salad packed with radish, cucumber, bean sprouts and Thai basil. The dressing is made from coconut cream, grated coconut, sambal and kencur, giving it a rich, creamy and spicy flavor. A recipe translated from Beb Vuyk's Groot Indonesisch Kookboek.

## Ingrediënten
- 100 grams of bean sprouts
- 1/2 cucumber
- 100 grams of young carrots (or sweet potato as substitute)
- 1/2 bunch of radishes
- 100 grams of coconut flour
- 2 tablespoons of shredded [[santen]] (coconut cream)
- 2 tablespoons of chopped onions
- 1 chopped clove of garlic
- 1 teaspoon [[sambal ulek]] (or 1/2 chili pepper finely chopped)
- pinch of [[kencur]]
- 2 tablespoons kitchen tamarind
- 1 tablespoon of fresh basil (or Thai basil)
- salt
- 1 teaspoon of sugar (optional, if using regular tamarind)
- a few drops of lemon juice

## Bereiding
1. Wash the vegetables, cut them finely and let them drain.
2. Rinse the bean sprouts under cold water. All vegetables remain raw in this dish (except sweet potato if used as substitute).
3. If using sweet potato instead of carrots, peel and cut into strips (young carrot size) and cook until just done but still slightly crunchy.
4. Rub onion, garlic, [[sambal ulek]] and [[kencur]] into a paste using a mortar.
5. Add the [[santen]] (coconut cream) and grated coconut to the paste and mix well.
6. Stir in the tamarind and salt (about 1 teaspoon total). If using regular tamarind, add a teaspoon of sugar.
7. Mix the thick sauce through the raw vegetables until everything is well coated.
8. Taste and adjust salt or sourness as needed. Add a few drops of lemon juice.
9. Add the fresh (Thai) basil and mix gently. If fresh basil is not available, use dried basil rubbed in with the onions.
10. Serve immediately.
