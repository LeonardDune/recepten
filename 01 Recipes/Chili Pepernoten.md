# Chili Pepernoten

> Chili Pepernoten zijn een pittige variant op de traditionele Nederlandse Sinterklaas lekkernij. De pepernoten zijn gemaakt met klassieke speculaaskruiden aangevuld met chilipoeier voor een extra kick. Het deeg wordt bij voorkeur een nacht in de koelkast bewaard zodat de kruiden goed intrekken.

## Ingrediënten
- 125 grams of self-raising flour
- 100 grams of dark brown caster sugar
- 50 grams of real butter
- 2 tablespoons of [[water]]
- 1.25 teaspoon of cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon clove
- 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
- 1/4 teaspoon of ginger
- 1/4 tsp coriander
- 1/4 tsp aniseed
- 1/4 tsp cardamom
- 3/4 tsp chili powder

## Bereiding
1. Mix the flour, dark brown caster sugar, butter, [[water]], and all spices (cinnamon, clove, nutmeg, ginger, coriander, aniseed, cardamom, and chili powder) together into a firm, somewhat dry dough ball.
2. Wrap the dough and place it in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes, preferably overnight, so the spices can fully infuse the dough.
3. Remove the dough from the fridge and let it rest on the kitchen counter for a while. Gently knead it a bit.
4. Take off small pieces of dough, knead each piece briefly for about 2 seconds, and roll into marble-sized balls.
5. Place the dough balls on an oven dish covered with parchment paper, keeping them roughly even in size.
6. Bake at 160 degrees Celsius for 14 minutes, adjusting time depending on size.
7. Let the pepernoten cool down on the baking tray. They come out tender but quickly become crispy. If they stay tender, bake them for another 5 minutes.
