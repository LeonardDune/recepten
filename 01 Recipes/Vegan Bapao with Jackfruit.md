# Vegan Bapao with Jackfruit

> Vegan bapao (Chinese gestoomde broodjes) gevuld met gekruide jackfruit (nangka) als vleesvervanging. Het deeg is tweekleurig gemaakt door een deel te kleuren met pandanextract, waardoor de broodjes een prachtig groen-wit uiterlijk krijgen. De jackfruit wordt gemarineerd in drie soorten sojasaus voor een rijke, hartige smaak.

## Ingrediënten
- 450 grams of raw [[jackfruit]] (from the freezer)
- 4 tablespoons sweet soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon of salty soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon of hot soy sauce
- 1 large onion
- 3 squeezed garlic cloves
- 2 spring onions
- 1/2 teaspoon of salt (for filling)
- 1/2 teaspoon of pepper
- 3-4 tablespoons of [[water]]
- 400 grams of plain flour
- 210 ml water (handwarm)
- 50 grams of sugar
- 3-4 tablespoons of sunflower oil
- 13 grams of yeast
- 8 grams of baking powder
- a pinch of salt (for dough)
- pandan extract

## Bereiding
1. Chop the [[jackfruit]] into fine pieces.
2. Squeeze the garlic cloves and chop the large onion.
3. Stir-fry the [[jackfruit]], onion and garlic in a large pan with some oil until the [[jackfruit]] gets some color.
4. Season with sweet soy sauce, salty soy sauce and hot soy sauce.
5. Add 3-4 tablespoons of [[water]], salt and pepper to the pan.
6. Lower the heat, place a lid on the pan and let the filling simmer for 5 minutes until the [[jackfruit]] is tender.
7. Add the spring onions, turn off the heat and mix through so the onions stay crisp.
8. Transfer the filling to a bowl and let it cool down completely.
9. Make the bapao dough by mixing flour, warm [[water]], sugar, sunflower oil, yeast, baking powder and a pinch of salt.
10. Knead the dough well for 5-10 minutes until it becomes shiny and firm.
11. Add pandan extract to one-third of the dough and knead until it turns green.
12. Divide both the green and white dough into 24 equal pieces each.
13. Roll the green dough into a sausage shape and flatten it, then place a roll of white dough on top and wrap the green dough around it.
14. Flatten the combined sausage and roll it out into a circle shape.
15. Add a spoonful of the cooled filling to the center of each dough circle and fold the bapao closed.
16. Place the bapao on parchment paper in a bamboo steamer basket.
17. Steam the bapao for 15 minutes until cooked through.
