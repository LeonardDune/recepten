# Daifuku Mochi with Anko (red bean filling)

> Daifuku mochi is a Japanese glutinous rice cake filled with sweet red bean paste (anko). This easy recipe uses canned aduki beans sweetened with gula Jawa and ketan flour for the mochi dough, prepared quickly in the microwave. The result is soft, chewy mochi parcels filled with a rich, sweet bean filling — a beloved Japanese treat traditionally enjoyed during New Year.

## Ingrediënten
- 250 grams of aduki beans from a can, drained
- 180 grams of [[gula jawa]]
- 120 ml of [[water]] (for anko)
- pinch of salt (if necessary)
- 100 grams of ketan flour
- 165 ml of water (for mochi dough)
- 70 g sugar
- 3 tablespoons of tapioca flour or corn flour (corn starch)

## Bereiding
1. Drain the aduki beans and rinse well under running [[water]].
2. Place beans on medium heat, add 120 ml of [[water]] and bring to the boil while stirring occasionally.
3. Meanwhile, melt 180 gr of [[gula jawa]] with a tablespoon of [[water]].
4. Add the melted [[gula jawa]] to the beans and simmer for 5 to 6 minutes while stirring, until it looks like thick porridge.
5. Pour the beans into a bowl and let cool. The paste will become firmer as it cools.
6. Once cooled, blend the anko with an immersion blender to a paste, leaving some bigger pieces if desired.
7. Mix ketan flour and sugar in a microwave-safe bowl.
8. While stirring with a whisk, pour in 165 ml of [[water]] little by little until you get a smooth, thin (milk-like) batter.
9. Heat the batter in the microwave for 40 seconds, then stir. Repeat heating (3x 40 seconds and 1x 20 seconds), stirring well between each session, until the dough is very sticky, thick and shiny.
10. Cover a baking sheet with baking paper and sprinkle generously with tapioca flour.
11. Scoop the hot dough onto the floured baking paper. With hands dusted in tapioca flour, flatten the dough to a thickness of 1.5 to 2 cm.
12. Cut circles from the dough using a cookie cutter.
13. Scoop a teaspoon of cooled anko in the middle of each circle and fold the dough closed (first top to bottom, then the sides).
14. Place each daifuku in a cupcake mold and serve on a dish.
15. Let the daifuku rest for at least an hour before serving. Do not refrigerate; freeze immediately if storing longer.
