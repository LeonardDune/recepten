# Sambal Kelapa - Indonesian Chili Salsa with Coconut

> Sambal Kelapa is a traditional Indonesian chili salsa made with grated coconut and a blend of aromatic spices including galangal, coriander, kencur, and shrimp paste. Unlike typical red chili sambals, this version resembles serundeng — a dry, spiced, toasted coconut condiment with a spicy kick. It pairs wonderfully with nasi goreng, Indonesian fish dishes, or sayur lodeh.

## Ingrediënten
- 100 grams of grated coconut
- 4 tablespoons of [[sambal terasi]] (shrimp paste)
- 1 teaspoon of [[laos]] (galangal)
- 2 teaspoons of [[ketumbar]] (coriander)
- 1/2 teaspoon [[kencur]]
- 2 teaspoons of Javanese sugar ([[gula jawa]])
- 4 jeruk purut leaves (kaffir lime leaves)
- 1/2 teaspoon of salt
- 2 tablespoons of oil

## Bereiding
1. Mix together the [[sambal terasi]] with the [[laos]], [[ketumbar]], [[kencur]], the sugar and the salt in a mortar and grind everything together well.
2. Make the oil hot in a skillet or wok with a thick bottom.
3. Remove the pan from the heat and stir in the spice mix.
4. Place the pan back on the lowest heat and add the grated coconut, stirring constantly.
5. Fry the sambal for a few minutes, stirring constantly until it turns yellow-brown in color. Do not let it get too dark.
6. Optionally add two tablespoons of water to loosen anything stuck to the bottom, making sure the sambal does not get too wet.
7. Remove the pan from the heat immediately once the desired color is reached.
8. Scoop the sambal kelapa onto a dish lined with parchment paper and let it cool down and dry overnight (or two nights if needed).
9. Transfer to a clean, well-sealed jar with a paper towel on the bottom to catch excess moisture.
10. Remove jeruk purut leaves before serving.
