# Perkedel Aceh (meatballs from Aceh)

> Perkedel Aceh are Indonesian meatballs from the Aceh region. Minced beef is mixed with roasted grated coconut and shaped into balls, then fried and served with a fragrant spice sauce made from onion, garlic, sambal, coriander, turmeric, tamarind and lemongrass.

## Ingrediënten
- 1/2 kg minced beef
- 5 tablespoons coconut flour (grated coconut)
- 4 tablespoons oil
- 3 tablespoons chopped onions
- 3 chopped garlic cloves
- 2 teaspoons [[sambal oelek]]
- 1 teaspoon coriander (ketumbar)
- 1 teaspoon turmeric (yellow root or kunjit)
- 3 tablespoons tamarind water (made with 3 tablespoons water and a piece of tamarind the size of one walnut)
- 1 lemongrass stalk
- salt
- pepper

## Bereiding
1. Rub onions, garlic, sambal, coriander and turmeric together to form a paste.
2. Add the tamarind water and the lemongrass to the paste.
3. Roast the coconut flour in a wok with 1 spoonful of oil, stirring constantly until it turns yellow.
4. Mix the roasted coconut with the minced meat until it forms a homogeneous mass. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Roll the meat mixture into evenly sized meatballs (ping pong ball size).
6. Fry the meatballs in oil in the wok until brown and cooked through, about 15 minutes.
7. Remove the meatballs and set aside. Sauté the herb paste in the remaining hot oil over high heat and let it reduce a bit. Taste and adjust seasoning.
8. Pour the herb sauce over the meatballs and serve.
