# Cha Soba Noodles with Shrimps

> A light, healthy and fast Asian-style noodle dish made with Japanese Cha Soba noodles and shrimps. The noodles have a subtle green tea flavor and are combined with a delicious mix of hoisin, mirin, fish sauce and sesame oil. Ready in just 20 minutes, perfect for lunch or a quick weeknight dinner.

## Ingrediënten
- half a pack of Cha [[soba noodles]]
- 100 grams of prawns (cooked)
- 1 garlic clove
- 2 teaspoons fish sauce
- 2 teaspoons [[mirin]]
- 2 tablespoons hoisin sauce
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- bit of crispy lettuce
- a handful of parsley
- 2 spring onions
- 4-5 cherry tomatoes
- chili pepper (optional)
- 1 tablespoon sunflower oil
- sesame seeds as garnish
- kikkoman soy sauce to serve

## Bereiding
1. Cook the noodles in boiling water for exactly 6 minutes.
2. Rinse the noodles immediately with cold water to stop the cooking process and prevent sticking.
3. In a tablespoon of oil, sauté finely chopped garlic, chili pepper and 1 chopped spring onion until the garlic starts to shine.
4. Add the cherry tomatoes and a teaspoon of finely chopped parsley.
5. Add the cooked shrimps to warm them up (or add fresh shrimps and stir-fry until cooked).
6. Add the sauces: hoisin sauce, [[mirin]], fish sauce and sesame oil. Stir for a minute.
7. Add the noodles to the pan using a pasta spoon and mix everything together until the noodles are warmed through.
8. Serve in a bowl with finely chopped lettuce, freshly chopped parsley and spring onions.
9. Place the seasoned noodles on top and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
10. Serve with kikkoman soy sauce on the side for extra saltiness.
