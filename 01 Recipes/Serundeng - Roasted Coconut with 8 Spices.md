# Serundeng | Roasted Coconut with 8 Spices

> Serundeng is een traditioneel Indonesisch bijgerecht van geroosterde geraspte kokos met 8 verschillende specerijen en pinda's. Het wordt gebruikt als smaakmaker, vergelijkbaar met gebakken uitjes. Het recept is gebaseerd op Beb Vuyk's Groot Indonesisch kookboek en levert genoeg voor één grote weckpot. De serundeng is minder zoet dan de kant-en-klare versies en de smaak van de kruiden komt goed naar voren. Het wordt beter naarmate het langer bewaard wordt.

## Ingrediënten
- 200 grams (2 cups) of grated coconut
- 3 to 4 tablespoons of peanuts
- 6 tablespoons of chopped onions
- 2 cloves of chopped garlic
- 2 teaspoons of [[laos]] powder (galangal)
- 4 teaspoons of coriander powder (ketumbar)
- 2 teaspoons of cumin powder (jinten)
- 1 teaspoon of salt
- 2 teaspoons of Javanese sugar
- tamarind the size of two walnuts (asem)
- 1/2 teaspoon Kaempferia galanga ([[kencur]])
- 2 jeruk purut leaves (lemon leaves)
- 4 salam leaves (Asian bay leaves)
- 1 stalk of lemon grass
- 3 tablespoons of oil

## Bereiding
1. Grind onion, garlic, galangal, coriander, cumin, Kaempferia galanga, salt and sugar together into a smooth paste.
2. Mix the tamarind with 2 tablespoons of warm water and add to the paste.
3. Heat up the oil in a cooking pan or wok. Fry the peanuts until they turn yellow. Remove the pan from the heat and take the peanuts out, leave them to cool.
4. Fry in the leftover oil the spice mixture until the onions are light yellow.
5. Add the grated coconut. Lower the heat and mix the spices well with the coconut.
6. Continue stirring, sliding from the middle of the pan over the bottom in the direction of the edge, until the coconut turns yellow.
7. Add the salam leaves and lemongrass and roast a little bit longer. Make sure the mixture does not stick to the pan.
8. When the mixture is evenly light brown in color, stir in the roasted peanuts.
9. Immediately place the serundeng on a plate on top of a paper towel. Let it cool completely.
10. Fill up an airtight jar. Leave the leaves and lemongrass in it. Serundeng stays fresh for months if stored dry.
