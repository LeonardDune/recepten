# Urapan with green beans, spinach and cabbage

> Urapan #II is an Indonesian salad with green beans, spinach, cabbage and bean sprouts, covered in a warm dressing of coconut cream and grated coconut. The dressing is flavored with sambal terasi, kencur and kaffir lime leaf, giving it an intense umami and fresh flavor. This dish is packed with fibers and minerals.

## Ingrediënten
- 100 grams of green beans
- 100 grams of grated coconut
- 100 grams of endive
- 2 tablespoons of shredded santen (coconut cream)
- 200 grams of spinach
- 100 grams of bean sprouts
- 100 grams of cabbage
- 0.5 dl [[water]]
- 2 tablespoons of chopped onions
- 1 chopped clove of garlic
- 1 teaspoon of [[sambal terasi]]
- 0.5 teaspoon [[kencur]]
- 1 kaffir lime leaf
- salt

## Bereiding
1. Rub onions, garlic, sambal, [[kencur]] and salt into a paste.
2. Bring the [[water]] to the boil, add the coconut cream and the spices.
3. Let it cook until it starts to thicken and then stir in the grated coconut.
4. In the meantime, cook the vegetables half-done with salt: first add the green beans to the pan, then add the cabbage cut in strips, after a minute or two add the spinach, and after 30 seconds add the bean sprouts and let simmer for another 30 seconds.
5. Rinse all vegetables with cold [[water]] to preserve their fresh green color.
6. Drain the vegetables and mix them gently with the warm coconut sauce.
7. Serve the urapan as a salad.
