# Orak Arik II (with shrimps)

> Orak Arik is a healthy and easy Indonesian vegetable dish made with shrimp. Various finely cut vegetables are stir-fried together with shrimp, spices and egg. The dish should remain crisp and is mildly spiced due to the use of deseeded chili peppers.

## Ingrediënten
- 100 grams of snow peas
- 100 grams of cauliflower
- 100 grams of carrots
- 100 grams of leek
- 2 tablespoons of chopped celery
- 100 grams of shrimp
- 2 eggs
- 2 tablespoons of oil
- 2 tablespoons of chopped onions
- 1 chopped garlic clove
- 2 chopped unseeded chilies
- pepper
- salt
- nutmeg
- pinch of monosodium glutamate ([[vetsin]])

## Bereiding
1. Cut all the vegetables very fine.
2. Slice or chop the shrimp also very fine.
3. Fry the spices in oil.
4. Add onion, garlic and lombok (chili peppers) and when the onions are yellow, add the shrimps.
5. Add the chopped vegetables and fry it together.
6. Beat eggs with salt, pepper and nutmeg and stir in when the vegetables are halfway done.
7. Keep stirring until the egg solidifies.
8. Mix in, just before serving, the celery and monosodium glutamate ([[vetsin]]).
