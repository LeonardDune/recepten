# Tahu Goreng – Fried Tofu (Central Java)

> Tahu Goreng is a classic fried tofu dish from Central Java, Indonesia. Slices of tofu are dipped in a spiced rice flour batter made with garlic, roasted candlenuts and coriander powder, then deep-fried until golden brown and crispy on the outside while remaining moist and tender on the inside.

## Ingrediënten
- 1 block of [[tofu]]
- 6 tablespoons of rice flour
- 6 tablespoons of [[water]]
- 2 deciliter oil (sunflower deep-frying oil)
- 3 cloves of garlic, shredded
- 5 candlenuts, roasted
- 1 teaspoon [[ketumbar]] (coriander powder)
- salt

## Bereiding
1. Cut the [[tofu]] into slices of one thumb thickness.
2. Grind the garlic, roasted candlenuts, [[ketumbar]] and salt into a paste using a mortar.
3. Mix the rice flour and [[water]] together into a smooth batter.
4. Stir the spice paste (bumbu) through the rice flour batter.
5. Dip the [[tofu]] slices into the batter and let them marinate for a minute, making sure they are completely covered.
6. Heat the oil in a deep pan.
7. Carefully slide the [[tofu]] slices into the hot oil and deep-fry two at a time for 3-5 minutes until golden brown and crispy.
8. Serve with red rice and sambal.
