# Satay Prentul (minced meat) or Satay Lilit

> Satay Prentul, also known as Satay Lilit, is an Indonesian grilled minced meat dish seasoned with a rich blend of spices and coconut cream. The meat is shaped around lemongrass stalks or bamboo skewers and grilled until cooked through. The lemongrass adds a lovely citrus flavor to the meat.

## Ingrediënten
- 1/2 kg beef minced meat
- 1/10 coconut cream
- 1 tablespoon of shredded onions
- 2 shredded cloves of garlic
- 1 teaspoon [[sambal trassi]] (chili salsa with shrimp paste)
- 1/2 teaspoon [[laos]] (galangal)
- 1 teaspoon [[ketumbar]] (coriander)
- 1/2 teaspoon [[jinten]] (cumin)
- 1/2 teaspoon [[kunjit]] (yellow root)
- 1/2 teaspoon of ginger powder
- pinch of [[kencur]]
- piece of [[asem]] (tamarind) the size of a walnut (deseeded and skins taken out)
- 1 stalk of [[sereh]] (lemongrass)
- pepper
- salt

## Bereiding
1. Rub the lemongrass ([[sereh]]) with salt until fine.
2. Add the other herbs: onions, garlic, [[sambal trassi]], [[laos]] (galangal), [[ketumbar]] (coriander), [[jinten]] (cumin), [[kunjit]] (yellow root), ginger powder, [[kencur]] and [[asem]] (tamarind). Work into a nice paste.
3. Mix the minced meat well with the coconut cream and knead until the coconut cream is almost completely dissolved into the meat.
4. Shape the meat mixture around lemongrass stalks, or make balls with a diameter of approximately 2.5 cm and place them on wooden or bamboo skewers.
5. Grill the satay on a BBQ or grill plate for about 15 to 20 minutes until the minced meat is cooked through.
6. Serve with pandan rice and Indonesian chili sauce.
