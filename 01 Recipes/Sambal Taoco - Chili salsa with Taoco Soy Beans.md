# Sambal Taoco - Chili salsa with Taoco Soy Beans

> Sambal Taoco is an Indonesian chili salsa made with fermented soybeans (taoco), chili peppers, onions, garlic, candlenuts, shrimp paste and tamarind. The taoco gives the sambal a silky, intense and umami-rich flavor. This recipe is based on recipe #39 from Beb Vuyk's Groot Indonesisch Kookboek.

## Ingrediënten
- 10 finely chopped [[lombok]]s (chili peppers)
- 100 grams finely chopped onions
- 3 cloves finely chopped garlic
- 5 pieces of [[kemiri]] (candlenuts)
- 1 teaspoon of Javanese sugar
- 1 teaspoon of [[terasi]] (shrimp paste)
- 2 tablespoons of [[taoco]]
- [[asem]] the size of a walnut (tamarind)
- 3 tablespoons of oil
- salt

## Bereiding
1. Crush the roasted [[kemiri]].
2. Rub onions, garlic, [[terasi]], salt and sugar together with the [[kemiri]] in a mortar.
3. Roast this mixture in the oil.
4. Add the finely chopped [[lombok]] and the [[taoco]] when the onions turn yellow.
5. Dilute the tamarind ([[asem]]) with 3 tablespoons of hot water to make [[asem]] water.
6. Add the [[asem]] water to the mixture and let it thicken for a few minutes, keeping the sambal slightly moist.
7. Boil everything intensely for at least 5 minutes before filling into a jar.
