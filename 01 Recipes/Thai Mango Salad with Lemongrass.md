# Thai Mango Salad with Lemongrass

> A delicious Thai mango salad with the perfect balance between sweet, sour and spicy. Made with fresh mango, lemongrass, red onion, chili pepper and a flavorful dressing of sesame oil, fish sauce, rice vinegar and soy sauce. Can be made with ripe or unripe mango. Optionally vegetarian by leaving out the fish sauce.

## Ingrediënten
- 1 ripe or unripe [[mango]]
- 1 large red onion
- 1 big garlic clove
- 1 lemongrass
- 1 red pepper
- half a lime
- a handful of fresh coriander
- two tablespoons of chives
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon fish sauce
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon salty soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar

## Bereiding
1. Cut the red onion into rings.
2. Cut the garlic and chili pepper into small pieces.
3. Cut the ripe [[mango]] into small strips.
4. Chop the lemongrass as finely as possible.
5. Chop the fresh coriander not too finely so the leaves are still visible.
6. Cut the chives into 1-centimeter pieces.
7. Mix the dressing ingredients together: sesame oil, fish sauce, rice vinegar, brown sugar and salty soy sauce.
8. Combine all salad ingredients and pour the dressing over the mixed salad.
9. Squeeze half a lime over everything.
10. Let the salad stand for about 10 minutes so the dressing flavors the [[mango]] and onions.
11. Mix the salad again just before serving.
