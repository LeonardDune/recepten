# Sambal Oel

> Sambal Oel is een halfverse Indonesische sambal met heldere, frisse smaken van jeruk purut, sereh en limoen. De umami komt van trassi en verse galangal. De sambal wordt half gegaard door er kokend hete (rijst)olie over te gieten. Het recept is vernoemd naar de man van de auteur, met de bijnaam Oel.

## Ingrediënten
- 5 [[chili peppers]] (preferably in multiple colors)
- 4 [[jeruk purut leaves]] (kaffirlime leaves)
- 5 cm fresh ginger
- 5 cm fresh [[galangal]]
- 2 cloves of garlic
- half stalk of lemongrass
- 1 teaspoon of [[trassi]] (shrimp paste)
- 1 teaspoon [[gula jawa]] (Javanese sugar)
- 1 teaspoon coriander seeds
- 1 teaspoon of salt
- pinch of [[kencur]] ([[kencur]] root powder)
- a quarter of a lime
- 1 teaspoon of tamarind
- 2 tablespoons of oil

## Bereiding
1. Finely chop the peppers, lemongrass, [[galangal]], ginger and garlic.
2. Remove the petiole and the midrib from the jeruk leaves and cut the leaves superfine.
3. Place all in a mortar, together with the [[trassi]] shrimp paste, [[gula jawa]], [[ketumbar]], salt and [[kencur]] and grind this together very well.
4. Add the tamarind and stir well.
5. Transfer everything to a heat resistant dish.
6. Heat up 2 tablespoons of oil in a pan until boiling hot.
7. Pour the boiling oil over the sambal in the heat resistant dish.
8. Squeeze the quarter lime over the sambal and serve.
