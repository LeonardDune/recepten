# Gourami dish with sea bass and sweet and sour sauce

> A crispy whole sea bass (or perch) fried golden brown with a light flour coating, topped with a sweet and sour ginger sauce. Based on recipe #216 from Beb Vuyk's Groot Indonesisch Kookboek, this Indonesian-inspired fish dish is ready in 30 minutes and serves 6 as a side dish.

## Ingrediënten
- 1 large sea bass (or perch)
- 2 tablespoons of flour
- oil
- pepper
- salt
- sweet and sour ginger sauce

## Bereiding
1. Clean and wash the fish under the cold water tap.
2. Make some cuts on the side of the fish on both sides and rub with salt and pepper.
3. Leave the fish seasoned with salt and pepper for about 10 minutes.
4. Pat the fish dry with paper towels.
5. Sprinkle the fish with flour and shake off the excess.
6. Heat up a generous layer of oil in a pan large enough to fit the complete fish.
7. Fry the fish on both sides until golden brown, about 5 minutes per side (10 minutes total). Check doneness through the cuts in the fish.
8. Let the fish drain on paper towels.
9. Top the fish with sweet and sour ginger sauce and serve.
