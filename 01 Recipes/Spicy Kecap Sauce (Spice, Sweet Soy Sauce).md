# Spicy Kecap Sauce (Spice, Sweet Soy Sauce)

> A spicy, sweet-sour Indonesian sauce made from kecap manis (sweet soy sauce), lemon juice, chili pepper, onions and garlic. Based on a recipe by cookbook writer Beb Vuyk. Delicious served over meat or fish such as pork chops.

## Ingrediënten
- 1 tablespoon of chopped onions
- 1 chopped garlic clove
- 1 chopped chili pepper unseeded
- 2 tablespoons of kecap (soy sauce)
- 1 tablespoon of vinegar or lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon of oil

## Bereiding
1. Rub onions, garlic and chili pepper together in a mortar or cobek until the mixture is tender but not too fine.
2. Sauté the spice mix in hot oil, keep rubbing the spice mix in the oil until the onions turn yellow.
3. Lower the heat and add the soy sauce and the vinegar or lemon juice.
4. Stir the sauce shortly and immediately pour it into a bowl. Make sure the sauce does not burn.
5. Taste the sauce and adjust with extra kecap or lemon juice if needed.
