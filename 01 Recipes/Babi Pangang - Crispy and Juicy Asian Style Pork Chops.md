# Babi Pangang - Crispy and Juicy Asian Style Pork Chops

> Babi Pangang is a classic Indonesian-Chinese style dish of crispy, well-marinated pork chops served with a sweet and sour sauce. The meat is marinated in soy sauce, stem ginger, and a 5-spices mix, then seared and oven-roasted until crispy on the outside and juicy on the inside. Traditionally served with white rice, cucumber acar, and krupuk.

## Ingrediënten
- 600-700 gram [[pork chops]] (about 4 pieces)
- 3 tablespoons [[salty soy sauce]] (Kikkoman)
- 4 tablespoons [[sweet soy sauce]] (Kecap ABC)
- 2 tablespoons sweet baby (stem) ginger
- 2 centimeters fresh [[ginger]]
- 1 [[onion]]
- 2 cloves of [[garlic]]
- 2 teaspoons [[5-spices mix]] (star anise, cloves, Chinese cinnamon, Sichuan pepper and fennel seeds)
- juice of 1/2 [[lemon]]
- 200 grams [[caster sugar]]
- 1 dl [[wine vinegar]]
- 1 cm ginger
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 3 tablespoons tomato puree
- 2 tablespoons water
- 1 chili pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- butter (for frying)

## Bereiding
1. Cut the bone out of the [[pork chops]].
2. Cut the [[onion]] into pieces and blend together with the [[garlic]] and finely chopped fresh [[ginger]] using an immersion blender or food processor.
3. In a large bowl, combine the [[sweet soy sauce]], [[salty soy sauce]], [[stem ginger]], [[5-spices mix]], and the blended [[onion]]-[[garlic]]-[[ginger]] mixture.
4. Add the [[pork chops]] to the bowl and mix well. Squeeze the juice of half a [[lemon]] over the meat and stir again.
5. Let the meat marinate for as long as possible, preferably overnight.
6. For the sauce: finely chop the [[chili pepper]] and [[ginger]]. Add all sauce ingredients except the [[cornstarch]] to a saucepan and warm over medium heat.
7. Once the sugar has melted, mix the [[cornstarch]] with one teaspoon of [[water]] to form a smooth paste and add it to the sauce.
8. Let the sauce simmer gently while stirring until it thickens. Remove from heat and set aside.
9. Preheat the oven to 200 degrees Celsius.
10. Remove the [[pork chops]] from the marinade and let the excess marinade drip off.
11. Sear the [[pork chops]] in a little [[butter]] in a pan for 1-2 minutes on each side until the outside is closed.
12. Place the seared [[pork chops]] on a rack with a drip tray underneath.
13. Bake in the middle of the oven for 15 minutes.
14. Turn on the fan and upper grill and grill for another 10 minutes until crispy. Keep an eye on them to prevent burning.
15. Remove from the oven and chop the meat into large strips or chunks.
16. Warm up the sauce and pour it over the meat just before serving.
