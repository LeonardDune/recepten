# Sambal Kemiri (Chili Salsa with Candle Nuts)

> Sambal Kemiri is a traditional Indonesian chili salsa made with candlenuts (kemiri), chili peppers, garlic, and salt. The candlenuts give the sambal a slightly greasy texture and mellow the spiciness. This recipe is translated from Beb Vuyk's Groot Indonesisch Kookboek and can be made in just 10 minutes.

## Ingrediënten
- 5 [[chili peppers]]
- 1/2 clove of garlic
- 5 candlenuts (or 2.5 teaspoon candlenut paste)
- 1 teaspoon of salt

## Bereiding
1. Rub the [[chili peppers]] with the garlic and salt finely in a mortar.
2. Roast the candlenuts above a gas flame on a knitting needle or metal satay pin, or in a tin foil box on an electric plate at the lowest position. Turn the nuts occasionally so they do not burn black.
3. While the candlenuts are still warm, rub them together with the [[chili peppers]], garlic, and salt until a smooth salsa forms.
4. Alternatively, add 2.5 teaspoons of candlenut paste to the mortar and rub together with the garlic, 5 peppers, and some salt.
5. Optionally deseed the peppers for a less spicy sambal.
