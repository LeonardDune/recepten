# Sambal Goreng Taoco (Bogor)

> Sambal Goreng Taoco is a spicy Indonesian dish from Bogor with a deep umami flavor derived from fermented yellow bean sauce (taoco). The dish combines meat, green beans, chili peppers and coconut cream into a rich, savory stew that is best served with white rice.

## Ingrediënten
- 100 grams of meat
- 100 grams of green beans
- 5 tablespoons of [[taoco]] (fermented yellow bean sauce)
- 3 tablespoons of sliced onions
- 1 clove of chopped up garlic
- 5 green lomboks (green chili peppers)
- 1/2 teaspoon of Javanese sugar
- 1 salam leaf
- 1/10 block [[santen]] (coconut cream)
- 4 tablespoons of oil

## Bereiding
1. Cut the meat into small cubes.
2. Wash and break the green beans.
3. Cut the green lomboks and fry them (without first rubbing them) together with the onions, garlic and the meat.
4. Add the green beans after a few minutes.
5. When the beans start to become tender, add a splash of water and the [[santen]] (coconut cream).
6. Once the [[santen]] is dissolved, add the [[taoco]] and the salam leaf.
7. Add the Javanese sugar.
8. Let the sambal goreng simmer until the meat is cooked and the sauce thickens, keeping the beans slightly crunchy.
