# Shrimps with Asparagus

> A classic Chinese recipe by Beb Vuyk for shrimps with asparagus. The shrimp are marinated in sherry and sautéed with ginger, then combined with asparagus in a soy-sherry sauce thickened with cornstarch. Best made with fresh asparagus during asparagus season in May and June.

## Ingrediënten
- 200 grams of shrimps
- 1 can of soup or leftover asparagus with asparagus broth
- 1 tablespoon of cornstarch
- 2 tablespoons of oil
- 2 slices of ginger root
- 1 tablespoon of soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons of [[sherry]]

## Bereiding
1. Sprinkle the shrimp with 1 tablespoon of [[sherry]] and leave them for some time.
2. Heat up the oil and fry the shrimp with grated gingerroot for 2 minutes.
3. Add the asparagus pieces and stir them in well.
4. Add 1 cup of the asparagus cooking liquid, [[sherry]] and soy sauce.
5. Bind the sauce together with the diluted cornstarch.
